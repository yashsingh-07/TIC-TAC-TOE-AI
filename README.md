🎯 How to Play

The board uses numbers from 1 to 9 to represent positions:

 1 | 2 | 3
---+---+---
 4 | 5 | 6
---+---+---
 7 | 8 | 9

You play as X.

The AI plays as O.

Enter the number corresponding to the position where you want to place your move.

---

🧠 Minimax Algorithm

The AI uses the Minimax algorithm to make its decisions.

The algorithm explores possible future game states and assigns scores to the outcomes.

Current Board
      ↓
Generate Possible Moves
      ↓
Simulate AI Move
      ↓
Simulate Human Responses
      ↓
Evaluate Possible Outcomes
      ↓
Choose Optimal Move

The AI tries to maximize its score while assuming that the human player will also make the best possible moves.

Because Tic-Tac-Toe has a small game state space, Minimax can examine the possible outcomes efficiently.

---

🏆 Game Outcomes

The game can end in three ways:

- Human wins
- AI wins
- Draw

The program automatically detects the result.
